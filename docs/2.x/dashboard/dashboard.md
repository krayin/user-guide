# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the first page you see after signing in. It reports on the CRM over a period you choose, and every section on it follows that same period.

### What the dashboard reports

**1) Overall-** The headline figures for the period.

**2) Total Leads-** How many leads were created.

**3) Open Leads by State-** Where the open leads currently sit.

**4) Revenue-** The revenue earned over the period.

**5) Revenue by Sources-** Which sources the revenue came from.

**6) Revenue by Types-** Which lead types the revenue came from.

**7) Top Selling Products-** The products sold most over the period.

**8) Top Persons-** The contacts bringing in the most revenue.

### Choosing the period

The period is set with the single date range control at the top of the dashboard. It carries both ends of the range on its face, so the period in use is visible at a glance.

**Step-1** Click the date range control to open it. A list of common periods appears beside a two month calendar.

**Step-2** Choose one of the periods from the list.

**1) Today**

**2) Yesterday**

**3) Last 30 Days**

**4) This Week**

**5) This Month**

**6) Last Month**

**7) Last 3 Months**

**8) Last 6 Months**

**9) This Year**

**Step-3** Or pick the range yourself on the calendar. Click the first date, then the last. Two months are shown side by side, so a range crossing a month boundary is picked without paging back and forth.

**Step-4** The dashboard reloads its figures for the period you chose.

**Note- A future date cannot be chosen, since there is nothing yet to report on.**

### Exporting the dashboard

Click **Export PDF** to download the dashboard as a PDF. The export covers the same period as the one shown on screen.
